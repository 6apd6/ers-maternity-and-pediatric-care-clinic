Nutrition Guide for Pregnant and Nursing Mothers

📅 July 31, 2026 | ⏱️ 5 min read

Proper nutrition is the foundation of a healthy pregnancy and successful breastfeeding. What you eat directly impacts your baby's growth and development.

Key Nutrients You Need

Folic Acid: Crucial for preventing neural tube defects. Found in leafy greens, citrus fruits, and fortified cereals.
Iron: Supports increased blood volume. Found in red meat, beans, and spinach.
Calcium: Essential for baby's bone development. Found in milk, yogurt, and cheese.

Foods to Avoid During Pregnancy

Avoid raw or undercooked meat and fish, unpasteurized dairy products, deli meats, and high-mercury fish like shark and swordfish. Limit caffeine to 200mg per day.

Nutrition While Breastfeeding

Breastfeeding mothers need an extra 450-500 calories per day. Focus on nutrient-dense foods, stay hydrated, and continue taking your prenatal vitamins.
