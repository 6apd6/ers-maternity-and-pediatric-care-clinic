Milestones to Watch: Your Child's Development in the First Year

📅 July 31, 2026 | ️ 5 min read

Watching your baby grow and hit new milestones is incredibly rewarding. Here is a general guide to what you can expect in your baby's first year.

Months 1-3: The Newborn Phase

Baby will start to lift their head during tummy time, follow objects with their eyes, and begin to smile socially. They will also start cooing and making vowel sounds.

Months 4-6: Getting Active

Expect rolling over (front to back and back to front), sitting with support, and reaching for toys. They will start babbling and laughing out loud.

Months 7-9: On the Move

Many babies start crawling or scooting. They can sit without support, transfer objects from hand to hand, and understand the word "no".

Months 10-12: Almost a Toddler

Baby may pull up to stand, take their first steps, and say simple words like "mama" or "dada". They will also start playing simple games like peek-a-boo.

Track Your Baby's Growth

Regular pediatric checkups help ensure your baby is hitting their milestones on track.
