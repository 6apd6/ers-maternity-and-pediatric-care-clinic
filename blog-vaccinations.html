Complete Vaccination Schedule for Your Baby

📅 July 31, 2026 | ⏱️ 5 min read

Vaccinations are one of the most important ways to protect your child from serious, preventable diseases. Here is a guide to the standard immunization schedule for infants in the Philippines.

At Birth

BCG Vaccine: Protects against tuberculosis.
Hepatitis B (HepB-1): Protects against liver infection.

6 Weeks Old

Pentavalent Vaccine (DPT-HepB-Hib): Protects against diphtheria, pertussis (whooping cough), tetanus, hepatitis B, and Hib.
OPV-1: Oral Polio Vaccine.
PCV-1: Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine.

10 & 14 Weeks Old

Second and third doses of Pentavalent, OPV, and PCV vaccines are given to build strong immunity.

6 Months Old

Influenza Vaccine: First dose of the flu shot. A second dose is given 4 weeks later.

9 Months Old

Measles Vaccine: Protects against measles, a highly contagious viral disease.
