Postpartum Recovery: What to Expect After Delivery

📅 July 31, 2026 | ⏱️ 6 min read

The postpartum period (the "fourth trimester") is a time of significant physical and emotional adjustment. Here is what you need to know about recovering after delivery.

Physical Recovery

Expect vaginal bleeding (lochia) for 4-6 weeks. If you had a C-section, follow your doctor's instructions for incision care. Perineal soreness is common; use ice packs and sitz baths for relief.

Emotional Changes

"Baby blues" are very common in the first two weeks due to hormonal shifts. If feelings of sadness, anxiety, or hopelessness persist beyond two weeks, you may be experiencing postpartum depression. Please reach out to us for support.

Breastfeeding Support

Breastfeeding can be challenging at first. Don't hesitate to ask our lactation consultants for help with latching, milk supply, or sore nipples.

When to Call the Doctor

Contact us immediately if you experience heavy bleeding (soaking a pad in an hour), fever, severe abdominal pain, or signs of infection.
