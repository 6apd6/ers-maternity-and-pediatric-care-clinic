Newborn Care Basics: A Guide for First-Time Parents

📅 July 31, 2026 | ️ 6 min read

Bringing a newborn home is one of the most joyous and overwhelming experiences for first-time parents. Here is a comprehensive guide to help you navigate the first few weeks with confidence.

1. Feeding Your Newborn

Whether you choose breastfeeding or formula feeding, newborns need to eat frequently—usually every 2 to 3 hours. Watch for hunger cues like rooting, sucking on hands, or fussing.

2. Umbilical Cord Care

Keep the umbilical cord stump clean and dry. Fold the diaper down so it doesn't cover the stump. It usually falls off within 1 to 3 weeks. Call your doctor if you see redness or pus.

3. Bathing and Hygiene

Until the umbilical cord falls off, give your baby sponge baths. Afterward, you can use a baby bathtub. Use mild, unscented baby soap and warm water. Never leave your baby unattended in the bath.

4. Safe Sleep Practices

Always place your baby on their back to sleep on a firm mattress with a fitted sheet. Keep the crib free of pillows, blankets, and toys to reduce the risk of SIDS (Sudden Infant Death Syndrome).

5. When to Call the Doctor

Contact your pediatrician immediately if your baby has a fever (100.4°F or higher), is unusually sleepy, refuses to feed, or has yellowing of the skin (jaundice).
