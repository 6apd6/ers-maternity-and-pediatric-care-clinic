Our Services

Comprehensive Care for Mother & Child

We offer a full range of maternity and pediatric services designed to support you through every stage of your journey

🤰

Prenatal Care

Comprehensive prenatal checkups, monitoring, and guidance throughout your pregnancy journey to ensure both mother and baby stay healthy.

  • Regular checkups and monitoring
  • Ultrasound services
  • Nutritional guidance
  • Pregnancy counseling
👶

Normal Delivery

Safe and gentle normal delivery services with experienced midwives and medical staff in a comfortable, supportive environment.

  • Experienced midwives
  • Comfortable birthing rooms
  • Pain management options
  • Immediate postpartum care

Cesarean Section

Professional C-section services when needed, performed by qualified OB-GYN specialists with proper post-operative care.

  • Qualified OB-GYN specialists
  • Modern surgical facilities
  • Post-operative monitoring
  • Recovery support
🍼

Pediatric Care

Complete healthcare services for newborns and children including checkups, vaccinations, and developmental monitoring.

  • Well-baby checkups
  • Vaccinations
  • Growth monitoring
  • Developmental assessments
💊

Postpartum Care

Dedicated post-delivery care for mothers including recovery monitoring, breastfeeding support, and wellness guidance.

  • Recovery monitoring
  • Breastfeeding support
  • Mental health screening
  • Wellness guidance
🩺

OB-GYN Consultation

Expert consultations with Dr. Evalyn Rivera-Castillo and Dr. Elli Sinsay for women's health issues and family planning.

  • Women's health issues
  • Family planning
  • Gynecological exams
  • Health counseling
